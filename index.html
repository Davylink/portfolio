ABOUT

I’m Davylink, a Technical Artist, 3D Artist and Game Developer working at the intersection of art, technology and game development. I'm a solodev, working across the entire production process, from 3D art, textures, animation, shaders and visual effects to gameplay programming, system design and optimization.

My work combines artistic vision and technical problem-solving to create complete and playable experiences. I also create tools and solutions that bring artistic ideas to life in games of different genres.

I have experience leading teams of programmers in the development of software solutions for companies. I organize team members by delegating tasks, overseeing their execution, and setting appropriate timeframes to ensure the process is carried out as effectively as possible.

I also have predisposition for learning new technologies, with a strong understanding of AI tools and their practical applications. I leverage them to optimize and streamline workflows, automate repetitive tasks and improve productivity.
